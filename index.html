How to get a better experiment
  • Use bright, steady lighting; avoid direct sunlight and flickering lights.

  • Remove strong filters; do not cover the camera.
  • Try again if the signal quality is low.

Important: This student demo uses a simplified camera-colour signal and is not clinically validated. It may show an incorrect estimate or no result. It cannot diagnose, screen for disease, or replace a medical device. Do not use its output to make health decisions.
